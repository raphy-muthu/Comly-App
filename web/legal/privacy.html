What we can tell you today

Comly collects the information you give us when you create an account and use the app. That includes your name, email address, date of birth, neighborhood, and — if you add one — a profile photo. Your date of birth is used to determine which jobs are safe and legal for you to apply to, and cannot be changed by you after sign-up.

Job posts include an approximate location only. Exact addresses and contact details stay hidden until a job application has been accepted by both sides.

If you enable push notifications, we store a device token so we can send them.

We do not sell personal information.

Questions before the full policy is published

Contact us through Help & Support in the app, and we will answer questions about your data in the meantime.
